Safer buying and selling on NextaStore

NextaStore helps buyers and independent sellers connect. Until wallet payments launch, the safest transaction is one where both sides agree on payment and fulfillment details before money or goods change hands.

For buyers

  • Don't send full payment before agreeing on delivery or pickup details with the seller.
  • Prefer partial or on-delivery payment where the seller offers it.
  • If you visit the seller, check that the item matches the listing before completing payment.
  • Keep communication inside NextaStore messaging so there is a record if something goes wrong.

For sellers

  • Don't ship or hand over goods before payment is confirmed by the method you and the buyer agreed on.
  • Keep product and order details accurate; a mismatch is the buyer's main protection.
  • Respond through NextaStore messaging so the conversation is documented.

What NextaStore does — and doesn't do

NextaStore verifies seller accounts and stores order requests. It currently does not process payment, hold funds in escrow, or guarantee delivery. Orders are requests sent separately to each seller, who confirms payment and fulfillment with the buyer.

Wallet and protected-payment features are planned for a future phase. Until then, use the order details, seller badges, and NextaStore messaging as your record.
