1. Who we are

NextaStore is operated by Nextawills Technologies (“we”, “us”). We decide how the personal data described here is used, and we handle it in line with Uganda’s Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash — we cannot read them.
  • Seller and store details: store name, description, logo and banner, store link, city or district, an optional precise map pin, contact phone number, payment methods you accept, and the products, prices and photos you list.
  • Orders: what was ordered, and the buyer’s name, phone number and delivery address for that order.
  • Messages: conversations between buyers and sellers, including the products and locations people share in them.
  • Notifications and activity: if you allow push notifications, a browser or device identifier we need to send them; and, where the feature is on, whether you are online so others can see your status.
  • Technical data: basic information such as IP address, browser and device type, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Subscription payments: for a Seller Pass, the payment reference and details you send us so we can check it.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account, run your store and process orders.
  • To let buyers and sellers contact each other, and to send you notifications about orders, messages, followed stores and your account.
  • To reach sellers about their store, subscription and support requests — this is why a phone number is required for sellers.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, review reports, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To fix problems and improve NextaStore.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • The public: a published store’s name, description, logo, products, city or district and store link. A seller’s phone number is public only if the seller chooses to show it.
  • Sellers see the name, phone number and delivery address a buyer gives on an order. Buyers see what a seller chooses to publish.
  • Conversation participants see the messages in that conversation.
  • Our team can access account and store information for support, safety and moderation, restricted by staff role.
  • Service providers that help us run NextaStore — email delivery, file and image storage, and hosting — only for those purposes.
  • Map services: when you use the location picker, your browser loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, and a dropped pin’s coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to suggest an address. The map library and icons load from public content networks.
  • Authorities: when the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

5. Push notifications

Push notifications are optional and only start after you allow them in your browser. Because a shared phone or computer may have more than one NextaStore account on it, a notification shows which account it is for. You can turn them off at any time in your browser or device settings.

6. Storage and security

We use standard protections: encrypted connections, hashed passwords, role-based staff access and automatic sign-out after a period of inactivity. Your browser keeps a sign-in session and some preferences in its local storage; we do not use this for advertising. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and sign out of shared devices.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you close your account we delete or anonymise personal data we no longer need, except records we must or reasonably should keep — for example order records that a buyer and seller may both need, and records needed to deal with fraud or a dispute.

8. Your choices and rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop us using it in a particular way. You can change most things yourself in your account settings, including whether your phone number is shown on your store. To make a request, contact us as below. If you are unhappy with how we handle it, you can also complain to Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office.

9. Children

NextaStore is not for children. Seller accounts are for people 18 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you think a child has given us their data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and, for meaningful changes, tell you in the app or by email.

11. Contact

Questions about this page, or a request about your account or data? Reach us through Nextawills Technologies, the company behind NextaStore, or message us from inside your NextaStore account.
