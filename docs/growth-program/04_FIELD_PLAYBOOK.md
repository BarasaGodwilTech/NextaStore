# NextaStore Crew field playbook

For Crew members, and for whoever trains them. Plain words on purpose: print it, put it in a WhatsApp group, read it aloud.

> Language note: everything here is English. Pitch cards in Luganda and other local languages would help a lot, but wording must be written or checked by a native speaker. Do not machine-translate them.

---

## The 60-second pitch

1. **Open with their problem, not our product.** "Do customers ever ask you for prices on WhatsApp, and you have to type the same thing again and again?"
2. **One sentence on what we are.** "NextaStore gives your shop its own online store with its own link. Customers see your products and prices, message you, and order."
3. **Remove the fear.** "It is free for 7 days. No card. You pay only if you like it, by MTN or Airtel."
4. **Ask for ten minutes now.** "Can I show you on your phone? We will have your first products up before I leave."

Never promise sales. Promise a tidy shop window customers can find and share.

## The five-minute demo (do it on **their** phone)

1. Open the sign-up link or scan your QR. The code is already filled in.
2. Create the account. Verify the email together.
3. Store name, a short description, area, phone number.
4. Add **three real products** with their own photos and prices. Three is the magic number: stores with three products pay far more often, and a store that still has 3 or more products when it renews earns you a stocked-store bonus.
5. Launch. Show them their store link, and send it to their own WhatsApp.
6. Say: "Share this link in your WhatsApp status today. That is your shop window."

## Answering "why should I pay?"

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I already sell on WhatsApp." | "Keep that. This puts every product and price in one link, so you stop re-sending photos. Customers can order without waiting for you to reply." |
| "Is it free?" | "The first 7 days are free. After that it is UGX 20,000 a month, and you can pay for more months at once." (check the current price on the Seller Pass page before you say it) |
| "I don't trust paying online." | "You pay by MTN or Airtel to NextaStore's official number, and send the transaction ID. A person confirms it. You are never asked for a PIN." |
| "I don't have time." | "That is why I am here. We will do it now, together, in ten minutes." |
| "Nobody will find me." | "Shoppers browse the marketplace, and you also get your own link to share. Stores with a badge are shown first, and 6 months of Seller Pass earns the Verified Seller badge." |
| "Let me think." | "Of course. Can I log your shop so no one else calls you, and come back Thursday?" (Then log the lead and **actually come back**: log the visit to keep your claim.) |

## Messages to copy (WhatsApp)

**After the first visit**
> Hello {name}, it was good meeting you today. Here is the link to open your NextaStore, it is free for 7 days: {link}  Use my code {code} so I can help you set up. I will pass by {day}.

**Signed up, no products yet**
> Hello {name}, your NextaStore account is ready. Let us add your first three products today, it takes about 10 minutes. When are you free?

**Store live, trial running**
> Hello {name}, your store {store} is live, well done. Share your link in your WhatsApp status today: {storelink}. Your free week is running, and I am here if you need help.

**Trial ending soon**
> Hello {name}, your free week ends in 2 days. To keep {store} visible, choose a Seller Pass on the Seller Pass page and pay with MTN or Airtel. I can sit with you to do it if you like.

**Payment sent, waiting for approval**
> Hello {name}, I saw you sent your payment. Please check the transaction ID you typed matches the one in your MTN/Airtel SMS exactly, so it is approved quickly.

(The Share studio has all of these ready, with your code already in. Copy one or send it to WhatsApp, and pick the contact yourself.)

## Your day

| Time | Do |
|---|---|
| Morning, 20 min | Open the dashboard. The top of **Your recruits** shows who needs you today: trials ending, stores with no products, delayed approvals. Message or call them first. |
| Midday | Walk the **Trail Map** area with the fewest live stores. Visit 6 to 10 businesses. |
| Each visit | Pitch, demo if they say yes, **log the lead before you leave** (so it is yours for 14 days). |
| Evening, 10 min | Log visits (a photo of the shop front and your location help if anyone ever disputes a lead). Reply to anyone who messaged. |

**Good targets:** shops that already sell by WhatsApp; boutiques, salons, phone and electronics sellers, food and bakery businesses, hardware, makers of crafts. Busy market stall owners with a smartphone and regular customers.

**Market Days:** when admin announces a campaign (for example +20% for payments in Kampala this weekend), tell stores in the pipeline. A store that pays inside the window earns you the boost.

## Your tools in the app

* **Invite card.** Share studio gives you a picture with your code and a QR, sized for a WhatsApp status. Post it every week. Tap **Share my invite card** or **Save as picture**.
* **QR on your phone.** Press **QR for my phone** and show it to the shop owner. It works even when you have no data, because it is made on your phone.
* **Messages.** Ready-to-send messages for every stage (after a visit, no products yet, trial ending, payment waiting). Copy, or send straight to WhatsApp.
* **Log a lead, even with no signal.** If you have no network, the lead is saved on your phone, you are told, and it is sent automatically when signal returns. It keeps the time you captured it (up to 48 hours back), so a bad network does not cost you your claim.
* **Trail Map.** Shows the districts where NextaStore has the fewest live stores, your own areas first. Walk there first.
* **Market Days.** When admin runs a campaign (for example +20% for Kampala this week) it appears on your page with your own count and a small leaderboard. Tell the stores in your pipeline.
* **Earnings estimator** (on the join page): slide the number of stores to see what a month could look like.

## How you earn (and how you do not)

* **First month:** your percentage of one month (25% at Trailhead, rising to 40% at Summit) when the store's **first Seller Pass payment is approved**. It **clears in a few days**, then you **withdraw**. Your dashboard shows every step.
* **Long-commitment bonus** when the store pays 6 months or more up front.
* **Stay bonus** when the store comes back and renews on time, plus a **stocked-store bonus** if it still has 3 or more products. Help them keep their shop fresh.
* Your rank goes up with paid stores in the last 90 days, and so does your percentage.
* **You are never paid for leads, visits or sign-ups.** Only for approved payments (and for stores that come back). So there is no gain in logging fake leads.
* **Rewards on a first payment never exceed 40% of what the store paid.** That keeps the program healthy for everyone.

## Getting paid

* Withdraw **UGX 15,000 or more** to your own MTN or Airtel number. **NextaStore pays the mobile money fee, so you receive the full amount.**
* We pay within **2 working days** of your request. You see the exact deadline when you ask.
* How often you can withdraw depends on your account: every 7 days at first, every 3 days once you are established, daily when you are Trusted.
* If you change your payout number, withdrawals pause for 48 hours for your safety.

## Rules that protect you and everyone else

* **Do** use your own code and help real shops.
* **Do not** sign up your own shop, a relative's account you control, or a friend's store you pay for. It is detected, the earning is cancelled, and your account is frozen.
* **Do not** pay a store's Seller Pass from your own mobile money to earn the commission. It is detected, and you lose money on it anyway.
* **Do not** promise things we do not offer, and never ask a shop owner for their PIN or password.
* **Do not** use another Crew member's lead. If a business is already claimed, move on. It is fair, and the claim ends in 14 days if they do nothing.
* Keep your payout number in **your own name**. If you change it, withdrawals pause for 48 hours for your safety.

## Getting stuck

* A store's payment has been waiting more than 24 hours: first check they entered the right transaction ID, then press **Ask us to check** on their card.
* A lead says "someone else already registered me": message support with the business name; the visit log settles it.
* Your balance looks wrong: open **Recent earnings**; each line says what it is for and when it clears.
