# Program design: NextaStore Circle and NextaStore Crew

Two programs, one engine.

| | **NextaStore Circle** | **NextaStore Crew** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | **The affiliate program** | **The field team** |
| Who | Anyone with a verified NextaStore account (buyers, sellers) | A vetted field team: people who walk markets and meet shop owners |
| Idea | "Bring a store into your circle." Word of mouth, made rewarding | "Find the stores worth finding." Paid, ranked, trained field work |
| Join | Instantly, after verifying email and accepting terms | Apply with phone, areas and an ID photo; an admin approves |
| Earns | A percentage of the store's first month, plus bonuses | A bigger percentage that rises with rank, plus bonuses |
| Tools | Code, link, invite card, recruit tracker | All of Circle, plus Lead Lock, visit log, offline leads, Trail Map, Market Days, pitch kit |

Why these names: a **circle** is the people around you, which is exactly who an affiliate invites. A **crew** goes out and brings stores in. Ranks follow a trail: **Trailhead, Pathfinder, Trailblazer, Summit**. None of this borrows from another company's program.

**How we say it.** The names stay. Every logo, page and message pairs each name with a plain descriptor so nobody has to guess what it is: **NextaStore Circle · Affiliate Program** and **NextaStore Crew · Field Team**. The descriptor lives in one place for the product (`PROGRAM_LABELS.descriptor` in `rules.js`) and in the logo files for the brand.

## 1. Decisions confirmed

* **The inviter earns the commission.** A percentage of the invited store's first month, once that store's payment is approved. (The two other readings of "percentage off the first month", a discount for the new store or a discount on the inviter's own Seller Pass, are parked as "for later".)
* **NextaStore pays the mobile money fee.** The partner receives the full amount they withdraw. Because every payout now costs us a fee, the rules favour fewer, larger payouts (see section 3).
* **The percentages must be worth it for NextaStore too.** The first version was too generous (a top Crew member could cost 70% of a month-one payment). Round 2 lowered the rates and added a hard margin guard (section 2).

## 2. The money rules (every number lives in `backend/src/growth/rules.js`)

Design rule: **pay less up front, more once the store has proved it stays.** A store that pays once and disappears costs us little; a store that comes back earns its partner the rest.

### What a store is worth to the inviter

| Line | Circle | Crew members | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| First month | **20%** of one month = UGX 4,000 | **25% / 30% / 35% / 40%** by rank = UGX 5,000 to 8,000 | The store's **first approved** payment |
| Long-commitment bonus | +UGX 3,000 | +UGX 5,000 | First payment covers 6 months or more (matches the Verified Seller badge) |
| Stay bonus | +UGX 3,000 | +UGX 4,000 | The store's **first on-time renewal** (once per store) |
| Stocked-store bonus | none | +UGX 2,000 | Paid with the stay bonus, when the store still has 3 or more products on its shelf |
| Market Day boost | +% set by admin | +% set by admin | Store pays while a campaign is live; the highest boost wins, boosts never stack |

The base is always **one month's price**, even when a store prepays a year, so the cost stays predictable. A long commitment is rewarded through the bonus, not through a giant percentage.

### The margin guard

**Everything paid on a first payment, all lines together, is capped at 40% of what the store actually paid.** So NextaStore always keeps at least 60% of any first payment, whatever the rank, bonuses or boosts. If the lines add up to more, the extras are trimmed first (boost, then long-commitment bonus) and the main commission last. This is tested against every rank, payment length and boost combination.

### What it costs you (generated by `scripts/growth-economics.js` from `rules.js`)

Run `node backend/scripts/growth-economics.js` after changing any rate to see the effect before you ship it.

| Store buys | Revenue | Circle | Crew member Trailhead | Crew member Summit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 month, never returns | 20,000 | **20%** (4,000) | **25%** (5,000) | **40%** (8,000) |
| 1 month, then renews | 40,000 | **18%** (7,000) | **28%** (11,000) | **35%** (14,000) |
| 6 months up front | 120,000 | **6%** (7,000) | **8%** (10,000) | **11%** (13,000) |
| 12 months up front | 240,000 | **3%** (7,000) | **4%** (10,000) | **5%** (13,000) |

For comparison, round 1 cost a one-month payer **30%** (Circle) and **up to 70%** (top Crew member). Worst case now is **40%**, only for a top-rank Crew member on a store that pays once and never returns, and the margin guard means it can never go higher. The typical case (a store that renews) is 18% to 35%.

Revenue here is Seller Pass payments only, so "we keep" is revenue kept, not profit. The admin **Economics** tab shows the real numbers as they happen (below).

### Crew member ranks

Rank follows **paid stores in the last 90 days**, so it is earned continuously.

| Rank | Paid stores / 90 days | First-month rate |
|---|---|---|
| Trailhead | 0 | 25% |
| Pathfinder | 5 | 30% |
| Trailblazer | 15 | 35% |
| Summit | 30 | 40% |

A promotion only lands while the Crew member's **quality score** is 60 or higher. Quality is behaviour, not volume: stores that pay, stay, renew, and are not flagged or reversed.

### Single level, always

No downlines, no overrides, no "recruit recruiters". You are paid only for stores you personally brought, and only for real payments.

### Is the program worth it? (the Economics tab)

An admin tab shows, for the last 30 days and since the start, split by Circle and Crew: Seller Pass revenue from referred stores, rewards, mobile money fees, **cost as a share of revenue**, paid stores and cost per paid store. It turns **amber at 30%** and **red at 40%**. It uses the **real fee** the admin enters when marking a payout paid, and the planning estimate only where that is missing. If it goes red, the levers are one-line changes in `rules.js`: lower the Crew member rates, raise the rank thresholds, or lower the upfront cap.

## 3. Payouts, and the mobile money fee we pay

* Minimum withdrawal **UGX 15,000** (up from 10,000), because a small payout wastes a fee.
* Withdrawals are spaced by trust: **New accounts every 7 days, Standard every 3, Trusted every 1.** Fewer payouts, fewer fees, and a natural fraud brake.
* Planning estimate of the fee we absorb: about UGX 700 on a UGX 15,000 payout (4.7%), falling to about 1% on large ones. **These fee numbers are my estimate, not the tariff.** MTN and Airtel publish theirs; correct `PAYOUT_FEE` in `rules.js`. The admin enters the actual fee on every payout, so the Economics tab is true to the shilling regardless.

## 4. Who gets credit for a store (attribution)

* **Link, code or QR.** Everyone shares one invite page, `https://circle.yourdomain.com/i/CREW-4HTR9` (or `…/invite?ref=CREW-4HTR9`). It tells the visitor who invited them (first name only) and sends them to sign up. The code is remembered for 30 days in their browser and sent with the sign-up form. It can also be typed in.
* **Late claim.** A seller who forgot can add a code within 7 days of signing up, as long as nothing has been paid.
* **One referrer per store**, enforced by a unique database column.
* **Lead Lock (Crew members).** Logging a prospect with their phone number makes it that Crew member's for 14 days, extendable twice by a week by logging a real follow-up visit. If the owner then signs up **without a code** and the store's phone matches, the lead holder gets credit. If the owner signs up with **another partner's code** during a live lock, the code owner gets the sign-up and an admin sees a "lead conflict" flag with the evidence.
* **Qualifying window.** The store's first payment must be approved within 60 days of sign-up, or the sign-up lapses.

## 5. The recruiter's view: following up so nothing stalls

Every recruit shows a status, a plain-language reason and a suggested next step. Recruits that need the recruiter float to the top.

| Stage | What the recruiter reads | Becomes "needs you" |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead logged** (Crew members) | "They have not created an account yet." | Claim ends in 3 days or less |
| **Signed up** | "Store not live." Tip: sit with them and add 3 products | 3 days with no live store |
| **Store is live** | "Free trial running." | Trial ends in 2 days, or 5 days live with no payment |
| **Payment sent, awaiting approval** | "We aim to approve within 24 hours." | After 24 hours: "longer than we aim" |
| **Paid** | "Approved, clears in N days" / "Ready to withdraw" | never |

Two buttons: **Send a nudge** (WhatsApp with a ready message for that stage; the recruiter picks the contact, we never show phone numbers) and **Ask us to check** (unlocks after 24 hours; creates an item in the existing Admin Follow-ups list; one per recruit per day). A once-a-day digest ("3 recruits need you today") reaches the bell, never more than one a day.

## 6. Strategies for reaching clients (all built and verified; see `06_STRATEGY_VERIFICATION.md`)

1. **Invite page.** A friendly landing page that says who invited you, explains NextaStore in three lines and carries the code through sign-up. The link every partner shares.
2. **Share Studio.** A portrait **invite card** (the partner's code and a QR code on a branded picture, sized for a WhatsApp status), downloadable or shareable in one tap, plus ready-to-send messages (status, direct message, group post) with the code and link already in.
3. **Offline QR.** The QR is generated on the phone, no internet and no external service. A Crew member can show it in a market with no data.
4. **Pitch kit (Crew members).** A 60-second pitch, a 5-minute demo on the owner's phone, answers to six common objections, and stage-by-stage follow-up messages, all in the app.
5. **Earnings estimator.** On the join pages: slide "stores in a month" and see what you could earn (one month / with renewals / 6 months up front). The best answer to "is it worth my time?", computed from the live rules.
6. **Lead Lock + offline leads (Crew members).** Log a business and it is yours for 14 days. **With no signal the lead is saved on the phone and sent automatically when signal returns, keeping the time it was captured** (up to 48 hours back, never future, never backdated). If a rival Crew member logged the same shop in the meantime, the earlier capture is kept as evidence for an admin.
7. **Trail Map (Crew members).** Districts ranked by how few live stores NextaStore has, the Crew member's own areas first, with their own footprint in each.
8. **Market Days.** An admin sets a boost for a window and place ("Kampala Market Week +20%"). Crew members and Circle members see it with their own count and a small **leaderboard** (first names only, within their own program). Time-boxed, visible, capped by the margin guard.
9. **Follow-up Radar.** The needs-you ordering and daily digest above, so field time goes where it converts.
10. **Seller-to-seller invite widget.** A drop-in card for the seller dashboard: "Know another seller? Invite them and earn", or the member's code and share buttons. Sellers are the best advocates NextaStore has.
11. **Rank ladder and quality score.** A visible trail with a countdown to the next rank, rewarding stores that stay.

### Later (deliberately not built)

* **Take it as Seller Pass credit** for sellers who invite sellers (touches the payment screen).
* **A discount for the new store** on its first payment (same).
* **Squads** with a shared Market Day bonus, and **weekly streaks**.
* **Local-language pitch cards** (need a native reviewer; none were invented).

## 7. What Crew members are not

Crew members are **independent partners paid per result**, not employees. They are not paid for visits, sign-ups or leads, only for approved payments (and, for the bonuses, only for stores that came back). That is the strongest anti-fraud decision in the design: there is nothing to gain from inventing activity. Have the terms (`05_PARTNER_TERMS_DRAFT.md`) reviewed by a lawyer before launch.
