## 1. Who we are and what this is
NextaStore [legal entity name, registered address] ("NextaStore") runs two referral programs: **NextaStore Circle** (open to any NextaStore user) and **NextaStore Crew** (a vetted field team). You are an **independent partner**, not an employee, agent or representative of NextaStore. Nothing here creates employment, partnership or any authority to bind NextaStore.

## 2. What you earn, and when
2.1 You may earn rewards for stores you personally refer ("Referred Stores"), at the rates and rules shown in your dashboard, which may change for future referrals with notice in the app.
2.2 A reward is earned only when **all** of these are true: the store signed up using your code, link or a lead you logged; it is a new store; **its first Seller Pass payment was approved by NextaStore** within [60] days of sign-up; and the payment passes our checks.
2.3 Rewards are held for a clearing period of [3] days after approval before they can be withdrawn.
2.3A The total of all rewards paid on a Referred Store's first payment will never exceed [40%] of the amount that store actually paid. If the rewards would add up to more, we reduce the smaller extras first.
2.3B Some rewards (a stay bonus when a Referred Store renews on time within [45] days of its coverage ending, and a stocked-store bonus for Crew members when it still has at least [3] products) are paid only if and when that happens, once per Referred Store.
2.4 **We do not pay for leads, visits or sign-ups.**
2.5 Single level only: you earn for stores you personally refer. There are no rewards for recruiting other partners.

## 3. Reversals
If a Referred Store's payment is reversed, refunded, found to be fraudulent or otherwise cancelled within [30] days of approval, the related reward is cancelled. If it was already paid, we may deduct it from your balance and future rewards until it is recovered.

## 4. Withdrawals
4.1 Minimum withdrawal [UGX 15,000]. Limits per withdrawal, per day and per 30 days apply, as does a minimum time between withdrawals (currently [7 days] for new accounts, [3 days] for established accounts and [1 day] for trusted accounts). Limits rise as your account builds a record with us and are shown in your dashboard.
4.2 We aim to pay within **two (2) working days** of your request, Monday to Friday in Kampala, excluding public holidays. This is a target for payment of a valid request, not a guarantee where we have asked you for more information or an account is under review.
4.3 We pay by MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money to the number in your own name that you registered. Changing that number pauses withdrawals for [48 hours]. We are not responsible for money sent to a number you gave us in error. **NextaStore pays the mobile money fee charged to send your payout, so you receive the full amount you withdraw.** [Confirm this wording, and whether it applies to every network and amount.] Any fee your own network charges you to cash out from your mobile money wallet is your own.
4.4 First withdrawals open [7] days after joining and after your first referred store has paid and cleared.

## 5. Fraud and misuse
5.1 You must not: refer yourself, a business you own or control, or an account you control; pay for a store with your own funds to earn a reward; create fake or duplicate accounts or leads; misrepresent NextaStore; ask anyone for their password or PIN; operate more than one partner account; use another partner's code or claimed lead; or use spam or misleading advertising.
5.2 We may withhold, cancel or reclaim rewards connected to suspected breaches, freeze your partner account while we review, and end your participation. We will tell you, and will give you a fair chance to explain where we can.
5.3 We run automated and manual checks (for example matching names, numbers, networks and devices). A check holding a reward is not a finding that you did anything wrong.

## 6. Lead claims (Crew members)
6.1 Logging a business with its phone number gives you a claim of [14] days, which you can extend by logging a genuine follow-up visit [twice, by 7 days each]. Claims end if the business is not pursued. If you log a lead without a network connection, the claim counts from the time you captured it (up to [48 hours] before it reached us), never from a time in the future. Another partner's earlier claim on our system takes priority, and we may review disputes using the records.
6.2 If two partners dispute a store, we decide using the records (lead logs, visits, photos, timestamps) and our decision is final for the program.

## 7. Your data and ours
7.1 For Crew members we collect your name, phone, a photo of your national ID, your areas, visit notes, optional photos and location points, and payout details, to verify you, run the program, prevent fraud and pay you. ID photos are stored privately and seen only by staff who review applications; [retention period].
7.2 You will see referred stores' names, area and progress, never their contact details, except details you gave us yourself about a lead you logged.
7.3 Handle any business owner's information you collect as personal data under Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019: use it only to offer NextaStore, tell them how their information will be used, and delete it when asked.
7.4 We process your personal data under our Privacy Policy [link].
