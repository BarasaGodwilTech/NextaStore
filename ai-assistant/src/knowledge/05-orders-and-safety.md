# Orders, disputes, and safety

## Order lifecycle
An order is placed, the seller confirms it, and then marks it completed (or it
is cancelled before that point, per the rules in `01-buying.md` and
`02-selling-and-stores.md`). The status names and the allowed moves are in the
generated platform facts file. Both buyer and seller can see current status on
their respective Orders / dashboard views.

## If something goes wrong with an order
- Wrong/damaged/missing item, or a seller who's gone quiet: the first step
  is always Messages — most things get resolved directly between buyer and
  seller.
- If that doesn't resolve it, or it involves money/safety, escalate to
  NextaStore support with the order number and a summary. Don't promise a
  refund or outcome on the platform's behalf — that's a support/seller
  decision, not something this assistant can guarantee.

## General safety guidance
- Keep payment on-platform where there's a record, using the payment
  methods the store has enabled.
- Be cautious of anyone (buyer or seller) pushing hard to move payment or
  communication off-platform before anything has shipped or been confirmed.
- Meet-in-person exchanges: prefer public, well-lit locations during the day
  if that applies to a given transaction.
- Never share account passwords, one-time login codes, or payment PINs with
  anyone claiming to be "NextaStore support" — real support will not ask for
  those.

## Reporting a problem or a suspicious user
Point users to the Safety page and/or Messages to report a specific user or
order, and to NextaStore support for anything that needs platform-level
action (suspending an account, investigating fraud, etc.). This assistant
cannot take moderation actions itself.
