# Seller Pass (seller subscription)

## What it is
aliases: what does the seller subscription do | how much is it for sellers | free trial for new stores | how long is the trial | do sellers pay anything | what keeps my store active | is it free to join as a seller | subscription price | how seller pass works | explain seller pass to me | what is the seller subscription about | what is seller pass | what does seller pass mean | how seller pass works for a new store
"Seller Pass" is what keeps a store active on the platform. Every new store
starts with a **free trial** period. The seller's subscription page shows
how many trial days are left. The price and trial length are provided by the live platform rules.

## Paying for coverage
aliases: how do i pay seller pass | pay for my store subscription | how to buy coverage months | send mobile money for seller pass | submit payment reference | why is my payment pending | how do i pay the monthly fee | subscription payment steps | how seller pass payment works step by step
This is about the SELLER paying NextaStore for the store's own coverage, not
about a buyer paying for an order. Once the trial ends (or a seller wants to
pay ahead of time), the seller buys coverage in month-blocks, paid via mobile
money (MTN MoMo or Airtel Money — see `03-payments.md`). The flow is:
1. Seller picks how many months of coverage to buy (the month-blocks on offer
   are in the generated platform facts file).
2. Seller sends the mobile money payment to the code/number shown, then
   submits the payment reference on the subscription page.
3. An admin reviews and approves the payment.
4. Once approved, the store's paid coverage is extended by that many months.

Payments show as **Pending** until an admin approves them, then flip to
**Approved** (or **Not confirmed** if rejected). This review step means
there can be a delay between sending money and the store showing as paid —
that's expected, not a bug.

## Cancelling or renewing a subscription, and what happens if coverage lapses
aliases: how to renew my subscription | cancel seller pass | does it renew automatically | what happens when coverage ends | subscription expired | refund for seller pass | when does my plan end | stop paying | what happens after the free days end | my free period is over
If a store's paid coverage (or trial) runs out before it's renewed, the
subscription status shows as ended and the seller is prompted to renew.
Renewing before then keeps the store active without interruption.

To cancel a Seller Pass subscription: there is nothing to cancel. Seller Pass
does not renew automatically, because it is prepaid month-blocks and not a
recurring subscription, and nothing is ever charged automatically. Coverage only grows when the seller sends a payment and an admin
approves it. Coverage simply ends on its end date.

To keep the store covered, the seller buys another block. A block bought while
the store is still covered is added after the current end date, so no time is
lost; one bought after coverage has ended starts from the day it is approved.
A seller who does not want to continue just does not buy another block.

If a seller stops paying, the store is hidden from shoppers once coverage ends,
and a seller badge shows only while the store is paid up. The seller can still
log in and see their own store. The seller gets in-app reminders when the trial
is about to end and when coverage has ended, and can buy a new block from the
subscription page at any time to bring the store back. For a question about a
payment already sent, use the Support link at the bottom of any page. The current refund policy is supplied by the live platform rules.

## Seller badge
aliases: badge tiers and months | when is the badge applied | does the badge disappear | badge needs continuous payment
Badge tiers are earned from continuous paid coverage: payments made while the
store is still covered add up, and a lapse starts the count again. The tiers and the months each needs are supplied by the live platform rules.

## What this assistant should not do
aliases: has my payment been approved | how many days are left on my trial | status of my seller pass | did admin approve my payment
Never tell a seller their payment has been approved, or guess a days-left
number — that status is live data on their subscription page, not something
in this knowledge base. Point them to the subscription page, or to support
if the page itself looks wrong. The current status and support contact are supplied by the live platform rules.
