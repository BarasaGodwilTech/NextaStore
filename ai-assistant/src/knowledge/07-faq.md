# Frequently asked questions

## Is NextaStore free to use as a buyer?
Yes — browsing and buying has no platform fee charged to the buyer through
the app itself. Selling involves the Seller Pass subscription described in
`04-subscriptions-seller-pass.md`.

## How do I become a seller?
Go through the store-creation/onboarding flow, which sets up the store basics,
branding and accepted payment methods, then launch the store once it has at
least one product. See `02-selling-and-stores.md`.

## Why can't I see MTN MoMo / Airtel Money / Card at checkout for a store?
That specific store hasn't enabled that method. It isn't a platform-wide
outage — try another accepted method or ask the seller via Messages. See
`03-payments.md`.

## How long does a payment approval take?
Seller Pass mobile-money payments need an admin to review and approve the
submitted reference before they count as paid — there's no fixed guaranteed
time for that in this knowledge base, so don't quote one; if it's taking
unusually long, suggest contacting support.

## Can I cancel an order after I have placed it?
Only for a short time after placing it, and only a limited number of times
(the exact window and limit are in the generated platform facts file). After
that, or once a seller has completed the order, ask the seller directly via
Messages, or escalate to support if needed.

## Does NextaStore have an app?
NextaStore is an installable Progressive Web App (PWA) — it can be added to
a phone's home screen from the browser and supports push notifications.

## What districts/areas does NextaStore support?
Stores set their own district/address and delivery details individually —
there isn't a single fixed platform-wide list to quote here. Point the user
to the specific store's page for its location/delivery info.
