# What to sell: ideas (advice)

> General advice and examples to spark ideas. These are NOT market data or promises of income, and not platform rules.

## How to pick a product idea
Choose something you understand, that people near you already buy, and that you can supply reliably at a profit. Check what similar stores on the [Marketplace](/marketplace) already offer, then ask how yours could be better: cleaner photos, better service, a more specific niche, or faster replies.

## Product ideas people in Uganda often look for
- Clothing and fashion: African print wear, made-to-measure tailoring, school and work wear, second-hand clothes in good condition, shoes, bags.
- Phones and accessories: cases, chargers, earphones, power banks.
- Home and kitchen: utensils, bedding, small appliances, decor.
- Food and drinks: baked goods, snacks, spices, honey, coffee, packaged items (follow food safety and local rules).
- Beauty and care: hair products, skincare, soaps, wigs and extensions.
- Handmade and crafts: baskets, beads, bark cloth items, gifts.
- School and office: stationery, books, uniforms.
- Baby and kids: clothing, toys, feeding items.
- Farm and garden: seedlings, tools, packaged produce (think about how fresh items will reach the buyer).

## Service ideas
NextaStore supports service listings too. Examples: tailoring and alterations, hair braiding, catering and cakes, photography, tutoring, repairs, cleaning, event decoration, graphic design. Say clearly where you work, how long a job takes and how you take bookings.

## Digital item ideas
E-books, templates, design files, study notes, short courses and tickets can be listed as digital items. Make clear what the buyer receives and how.

## Ideas for a small budget
- Start with items you can buy or make in small quantities.
- Take orders first and buy or make after the buyer confirms (made-to-order), so you are not stuck with stock.
- Offer a service where your skill is the main cost.
- Reinvest early profits into your best sellers.

## If you already sell offline
Bring your best 5 sellers first, photograph them well, and tell your regular customers about your store link.

## How to narrow down
Ask the person: what do you enjoy or know well, how much can you start with, and where are you based? Then suggest 3 to 5 ideas that fit and say why.
