# Shopping tips for buyers (advice)

> General shopping advice. Platform safety rules are in the orders and safety file; those take priority.

## Finding what you need
Search from the [Marketplace](/marketplace), browse by category, or look through [Stores](/stores). Save items you like to [Favorites](/favorites) and follow stores you trust to see their new products.

## Choosing a trustworthy seller
- Look for a complete store profile: logo, clear description, real location and clear photos.
- Check for seller badges, which are a trust signal.
- Message the seller before buying to ask about size, condition, delivery time and price.
- Be careful if prices look far too good to be true.

## Comparing fairly
Compare the same item across a few stores. Include delivery or transport in the total, and compare condition and what is included, not only the price.

## Gift ideas: where to start
Ask yourself who the gift is for, the occasion and your budget. Handmade items, hampers, clothing made to measure, accessories, books and treats are often good starting points; look in the category pages and ask a seller if they can wrap or personalise.

## Staying safe
Keep payment and chat inside NextaStore, never share passwords or OTP codes, and check [Trust and Safety](/safety) if anything feels wrong.
