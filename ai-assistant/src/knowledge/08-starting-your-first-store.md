# Starting your first store (advice)

> Mixed file: steps that name NextaStore screens are platform facts; the rest is general advice, not platform rules.

## The simple path to your first store
1. Create an account and choose to sell ([Open a store](/signup)).
2. Go through store setup. It has a few short steps: tell us about your store, add your branding (logo and banner), choose how customers will pay you, then review and launch. Add at least one listing so you can launch.
3. Add your first listings from your dashboard. A listing can be a product, a service or a digital item.
4. Share your store link with people you know.
New stores can try NextaStore before paying; see the Seller Pass notes for the details.

## Before you launch: a quick checklist
- A store name that is easy to say and spell, and a short description of what you sell and where you are.
- A clear logo (a photo of your shop sign or your initials is fine).
- At least 5 listings with clear photos, honest prices and short descriptions. A store with a few good listings looks more trustworthy than one with many unclear ones.
- Contact details and a way to receive payment that you check often.
- Know how you will deliver or hand over each item and what it costs the buyer.

## Choosing what to sell first
Start with what you already know, already have access to, or what people around you keep asking you for. Ask yourself:
1. Do people near me already buy this regularly?
2. Can I get or make it again and again at a price that leaves me a profit?
3. Is it easy to photograph and describe?
Begin with 1 category and 5 to 10 items. Learn what sells, then add more.

## Your first week after launch
- Share your store link in WhatsApp status and groups you belong to, and on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok if you use them.
- Tell friends and family, and ask for honest feedback on your photos and prices.
- Reply to messages quickly, even if it is only "I will check and get back to you today".
- Add one new listing every few days; followers are notified of new products.

## Common first-store mistakes
- Blurry or dark photos, or photos taken from the internet instead of your own item.
- Prices that forget transport, packaging or your own time.
- Listing items you cannot restock or cannot get quickly.
- Slow replies, or asking buyers to pay outside the platform.
- Too many categories too soon.
