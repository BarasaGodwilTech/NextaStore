# Account, messaging, and notifications

## Account basics
aliases: how do i log in | how to log out | stay logged in | manage my account | change my details | profile settings | where is account settings | how to log in to my account | where is the login page
Sign up, log in, and "forgot password" are handled from their own pages.
Sessions can be "remembered" (stay logged in longer) or standard. A user can
log out of just this device, or (where available) all devices at once.

## Signing up and creating an account (how to sign up, sign-up, signup, register, join)
aliases: how do i create an account | register on nextastore | join the platform | what do i need to sign up | password rules for signup | do i need a store to sign up | become a seller later | already have an account | create profile
Anyone can create an account from the Sign up page, and a shopper does not need a store. The steps are:
1. Choose what you want to do: **Shop** (browse and buy from sellers) or **Sell** (set up your own store).
2. Enter your full name and your email address, then choose a strong password that meets the requirements shown on the sign-up form and type it a second time to confirm it. The current password-length requirement is supplied by the live platform rules.
3. Tick the box to agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy. The account cannot be created without it.
4. Press the button to create the account. You are signed in straight away.

A Shop account opens the Marketplace. A Sell account opens the store set-up steps, and every new store starts with a free trial of Seller Pass. A shopper who decides to sell later can choose **Become a seller** in the account menu. If the email address already has an account, sign-up says so: log in instead, or use "Forgot password?" on the Log in page.

## Verifying your email
aliases: verify my email | verification link did not arrive | no verification email | confirm my email address | how do i get the verify email | email not coming | verification link no longer works | verify link has timed out
Sign-up does not send a verification email by itself, and the app does not lock anything until the email is verified. To get the email, open the account menu and choose **Verify email**, which is shown only while the email is not yet verified. The app then tells you to check your inbox for a verification link.

Opening the link shows a page that says the email is verified, with a Continue button to the Marketplace. A link can be used once and expires after a while. Asking for a new email makes any older unused link stop being valid, so use the newest one. If the page says the link is invalid or expired, ask for a new email the same way.

If no email arrives, use the Support link at the bottom of any page. The current e-mail delivery status and support contact are supplied by the live platform rules.

## Resetting a forgotten password
aliases: cannot log in password wrong | how to recover my account | password reset link | change password if i forgot it | locked out of my account | the reset email did not come
1. On the Log in page, choose **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address on the account and send the reset link. The page gives the same reply whether or not an account exists with that email: "If that email exists, a reset link is on its way."
3. Open the reset link from the email. It opens the same page with a box for a new password that meets the requirements shown on the reset form.
4. Save the new password, then log in with it.

A reset link can be used once and expires after a while. Asking for another link makes the older one stop being valid. Resetting a password logs the account out on every device, so log in again on each one. If no reset e-mail arrives, use the Support link at the bottom of any page. The current e-mail delivery status and support contact are supplied by the live platform rules.

## Remember me and how long a login lasts
aliases: keep me logged in | why do i get logged out | log out of all devices | stay signed in | session length | sign out everywhere | remember me tick box | logged out on every device
The Log in page has a **Remember me** tick box. Ticked, the login is kept for a longer session and survives closing the browser. Unticked, the session is shorter and ends when the browser tab is closed. In both cases the session is extended while the person keeps using the site.

A brand-new account has no tick box and behaves like an unticked login. A seller can log out everywhere with **Log out of all devices** in the account settings of the seller dashboard.

## Messaging
aliases: how do i message a seller | chat with a seller | where are my messages | open my inbox | talk to a buyer | send a message about a product | is the seller online | read my chats | ask the seller about an item before i buy it
Buyers and sellers message each other directly — about a product before
buying, about an order after buying, or general questions. A presence
indicator shows when the other person is online. Message threads are tied
to the relevant store/product/order context where applicable.

## Notifications
aliases: bell icon | push notifications | how do i get alerts | turn on notifications | what notifications will i get | why am i not getting notifications | install the app on my phone | get alerted about new messages | be told when my order changes
In-app notifications cover things like: order status changes, new messages,
new products from stores a buyer follows, and subscription reminders for
sellers (e.g. trial ending soon). Push notifications mirror the important
ones to the browser/device if the user has enabled push and installed the
app (NextaStore works as an installable PWA).

## Account safety
aliases: account compromised | how to secure my account | change password after hack | was my password stolen | what do i do if someone logged in as me
- If a user suspects their account is compromised, the priority is changing
  the password and, if available, logging out of all other devices.
- This assistant should never ask a user to paste their password, OTP, or
  full payment details into the chat — if a question seems to be heading
  that way, redirect to the real account/security page instead of
  collecting sensitive data in the chat itself.
