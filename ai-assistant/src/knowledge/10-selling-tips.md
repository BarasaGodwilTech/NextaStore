# Selling tips (advice)

> General business advice. Not platform rules and not guaranteed results. Platform facts about badges, fees or the Seller Pass are in the other files.

## How to price your products
aliases: how do i set prices | what price should i put | how much should i charge | calculate my profit | how do i decide the price of an item | how much profit to add
- Work out your full cost: what you paid or spent to make the item, packaging, transport, and a fair amount for your time.
- Add the profit you need on top.
- Compare similar items on the [Marketplace](/marketplace) so you are not far above or below the usual range, and be ready to explain why yours is different.
- Keep discounts honest; only show an original price that was really your price.
- Review your prices now and then as your costs change.

## How to take good product photos that sell
aliases: take better pictures | photo tips | make my photos look professional | how to photograph my items | good pictures for products | what background to use for photos
- Use daylight near a window, and wipe the phone lens first.
- Use a plain, uncluttered background.
- Take 3 to 5 photos: front, side, close-up of the detail, and one showing size (next to a common object or worn).
- Keep your photo style consistent across the store.
- Use your own photos of the real item.

## Writing a good description
aliases: how to describe my item | description tips | what details to include about a product
Say what it is, size or measurements, colour, material, condition, what is included, how delivery or pickup works, and who it suits. Use plain words. Mixing English and Luganda is fine if your customers speak that way.

## Earning trust as a new seller
aliases: how do i get buyers to trust me | build trust as a seller | look trustworthy | how to be a credible seller | why buyers should trust my store
- Complete your store profile with logo, banner, description and location.
- Keep stock and prices up to date.
- Reply fast and politely, and keep your promises on delivery times.
- Keep conversations in NextaStore Messages so there is a record, and agree price and delivery or pickup there before any payment.
- Be patient: trust builds order by order.

## Getting your first customers
aliases: how do i get my first buyers | find customers | get more customers | how to get sales | no one is buying | attract buyers to my store | get my first order
- Share your store link in WhatsApp status and groups, and on social media.
- Ask friends and family to look, follow your store and share it.
- Post new items regularly so followers see something fresh.
- Ask happy buyers to tell others about you.

## Handling orders well
aliases: reply to buyers quickly | best way to handle an order | package items | keep customers happy | what to do when i get an order
Confirm quickly, tell the buyer what happens next, update them through Messages if anything changes, and package items neatly.

## Growing steadily
aliases: how to grow my store | scale my shop | increase sales over time | restock best sellers | busy seasons for selling | busy seasons | festive period sales | back to school demand | when do sales go up
Restock your best sellers, add items close to what already sells, and plan for busy seasons such as back-to-school and festive periods.
