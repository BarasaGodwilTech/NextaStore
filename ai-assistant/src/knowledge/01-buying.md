# Buying on NextaStore

## Finding products
aliases: how to search for things | where do i look for items to buy | browse by category | find a product i want | how to shop here | how do i look for something | search bar and filters
Buyers can browse the Marketplace page, search, and filter by category. Each
store also has its own store page listing only that seller's products.

## Product detail page
aliases: what is on a product page | how to ask the seller a question about an item before buying | see how many sold | stock status of an item | add item to cart from product page | can i message the seller from the product
Shows price, description, images, how many have sold, and stock status. From
here a buyer can add the item to their cart, favorite it for later, follow
the store, or message the seller directly with questions before buying.

## Cart and checkout
aliases: how to buy something | how do i check out | what happens at checkout | pick delivery or pickup | can i order without an account | do i need to sign up to buy | how do i place my order | where is my cart | choose a payment method when buying | ordering from more than one store at once | items from several stores in one cart | one checkout for two sellers | does each seller get their own order
Items sit in the cart until checkout. A cart can hold items from more than one
store, and each store's seller then gets a separate order. At checkout the
buyer picks a delivery
district/address and a payment method. Only payment methods that are (a)
currently enabled platform-wide by an admin, and (b) accepted by that
specific store, are shown as options — see `03-payments.md` for how that
works. If a buyer doesn't see a payment method they expected, it likely
means that particular store hasn't turned it on, not that it's broken.

## Placing and tracking an order
aliases: where can i see my orders | how do i know my order status | track my purchase | where is my order | see orders i made | what happens after i order | order updates and notifications | my order is stuck on a status | has my order arrived yet | when will my order get to me
After checkout, the order appears on the buyer's Orders page with a status
that updates as the seller processes it. Buyers get a notification (and push
notification, if enabled) on status changes. The exact status names and what
each one is shown as are in the generated platform facts file.

## Cancelling an order (buyer side)
aliases: i ordered by mistake cancel | undo my order | cancel after placing | can i get out of an order | how long to cancel an order | change my mind about an order
A buyer can cancel their own order from the Orders page, but only for a short
time after placing it, only while the seller has not finished it, with a short
reason, and only a limited number of times. The exact window and limit are in
the generated platform facts file; quote those, never guess. Once the window has
passed, or the limit is used, contact the seller directly through Messages and
ask them to cancel.

## Favorites and Following
aliases: how to save a product for later | like an item | wishlist | what does follow do | get notified about a store | saved items page | difference between saving and following | where are the items i hearted | my liked items page
- **Favorites** save individual products for later — visible on the
  Favorites page.
- **Following** subscribes a buyer to a store — followers get notified when
  that store adds a new product.

## Safety while buying
aliases: how do i avoid scammers | pay safely to a seller | what if the seller asks me to pay outside | protect myself when shopping | seller asks to talk on whatsapp | is my money safe | do you hold my money
See `05-orders-and-safety.md`. In short: NextaStore does not process or hold
your payment, so agree the price, delivery or pickup details and timing with
the seller in NextaStore Messages first, so there is a record. Be cautious of
any seller who pushes you to leave NextaStore Messages, or to pay in a hurry,
before those details are agreed.
