1. Who we are and what these terms cover

NextaStore is an online marketplace and store-builder for sellers and shoppers in Uganda, operated by Nextawills Technologies (“NextaStore”, “we”, “us”). These terms are the agreement between you and us when you create an account or use nextastores.com and the NextaStore app.

NextaStore is a platform. Sellers run their own stores and set their own products, prices and delivery arrangements. When you buy something, the sale is between you and that seller — we are not the seller and we are not a party to it.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older to open a seller account. If you are younger and shopping, do it with a parent or guardian’s permission.
  • Give us accurate details (a real name, a working email address and, for sellers, a phone number we can reach you on) and keep them up to date.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Keep your password private, and use “Log out of all devices” in your settings if you think someone else has it.
  • One person, one account. Don’t create accounts to get around a suspension or to impersonate someone.

3. Buying on NextaStore

  • An order is a request. When you place an order, each seller receives a separate pending order request. It becomes a sale only when the seller confirms it.
  • NextaStore does not process your payment. We do not hold money in escrow and we do not guarantee delivery, quality or that an item matches its listing. You agree payment and delivery or pickup directly with the seller, using the methods they show on their store (for example MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, cash on delivery or pickup).
  • Before you pay, agree the price, delivery or pickup details and timing in NextaStore messaging so there is a record. Our Trust & Safety page has practical tips.
  • You can cancel a pending order from your orders page. Refunds and returns are handled between you and the seller.

4. Selling on NextaStore

  • Your store, your responsibility. You are responsible for your products, prices (in UGX), stock, descriptions, photos, delivery, customer service and any taxes or licences that apply to your business.
  • Accurate listings. Photos and descriptions must be honest and match what you deliver. A mismatch is the buyer’s main protection, and repeated ones can cost you your store.
  • Confirm before you hand over goods. Don’t ship or release goods until payment is confirmed by the method you and the buyer agreed.
  • Your phone number. We require a phone number so we can follow up with you. It is shown on your public store only if you choose to show it, and you can change that at any time in Settings.
  • Going live. A store needs a name, description, location (at least a city or district), a phone number and at least one product before it can be published.
  • Seller Pass. New stores get a free trial. After that, keeping a store active needs a Seller Pass. Prices, trial length and coverage periods are shown on the Subscription page when you pay, and a payment you send is checked before it is recorded. If a store’s coverage ends, some store features may be limited until it is renewed.

5. Your store link

Every store gets a link like nextastores.com/your-store. You choose the last part when you set up, and you can change it later, but changing it means old links you have already shared stop working. We may change or remove a link that impersonates another person or brand, is misleading, or breaks these terms. Links may move to another domain (for example nextastore.ug) in future; we will redirect them where we can.

By uploading it you give us a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to store, display, resize and distribute it as needed to run NextaStore (for example showing your products in the marketplace, in search results and in notifications). Only upload things you have the right to use. If you upload someone else’s work, you are responsible for it.

7. What is not allowed

  • Selling illegal, stolen, counterfeit, dangerous or age-restricted goods, or anything you are not permitted to sell in Uganda.
  • Fraud, fake orders, fake listings or taking payment for goods you do not intend to deliver.
  • Harassing, threatening or deceiving other people, including in messages.
  • Scraping the service, probing it for weaknesses, interfering with it, or trying to access accounts that are not yours.
  • Moving a conversation or payment off the platform to avoid a dispute record after a problem has started.

8. Reports, moderation and suspension

You can report a store, product, message or order. We may review reports, remove content, limit features, or suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put other people at risk. Where it is safe and practical we will tell you why. If you think we got it wrong, contact us and we will look again.

We work hard to keep NextaStore running and accurate, but it is provided “as is”. Because sellers, not NextaStore, sell and deliver the goods, we are not responsible for a seller’s products, a buyer’s conduct, or a deal that goes wrong between them. To the extent the law allows, our liability to you for anything arising from your use of NextaStore is limited to the amount you paid us (for example for a Seller Pass) in the 12 months before the problem. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited, or your rights as a consumer under Ugandan law.

10. Ending your account

You can stop using NextaStore at any time and ask us to close your account. We can suspend or close an account that breaks these terms. Some information (for example completed order records that both sides may need) may be kept after closure as described in our Privacy Policy.

12. Governing law
