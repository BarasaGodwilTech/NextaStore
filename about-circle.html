    What is NextaStore Circle?

    A circle is the people around you. NextaStore Circle turns word of mouth into income: anyone with a verified NextaStore account can invite a store owner, and earns a reward when that store pays for its Seller Pass. You are never selling for us. You are simply introducing people to a tool you believe in.

    Open to anyone

    Buyers and sellers alike. You only need a verified NextaStore account and to accept the program terms.

    Paid for real results

    You earn when a store you invited actually pays, never just for a sign-up.

    Simple and single level

    You are paid only for stores you personally brought. No recruiting chains, no downlines.

    Tools included

    Your own code, link and a shareable invite card with a QR code, plus a tracker that shows where each store is.

    How it works

    1. Join the CircleVerify your email, accept the terms, and get your personal invite code and link instantly.
    2. Share your inviteSend your link or invite card on WhatsApp, or let the store owner type your code when signing up.
    3. They open a storeThe owner signs up, adds products and goes live. A free trial of a few days lets them see it work first.
    4. You earnWhen their first Seller Pass payment is approved, your reward is added to your balance.

    What you can earn

    The Seller Pass costs a small monthly fee a month. Your rewards are based on one month's price, even if the store pays for a whole year up front.

    RewardWhen it is paidYou earn
    First-month rewardThe store's first approved paymentA percentage (of one month)
    Long-commitment bonusFirst payment covers several months or more+ A bonus
    Stay bonusThe store's first on-time renewal, paid within a set number of days after its paid period ends+ A bonus

    Example: a store you invite pays for one month and later renews on time. You earn the first-month reward plus the stay bonus, a combined total in all. Special Market Day boosts may add more while a campaign is live.

    Getting paid

    Rewards are paid to your mobile money number (MTN or Airtel). A reward is held for a few days after the store's payment is approved, so it can clear. Then it is yours to withdraw.

    Minimum withdrawal

    You can withdraw from a small minimum. Fewer, larger payouts keep things fast and fair for everyone.

    Paid within a few working days

    Once you request a withdrawal, it is paid to your number within a few working days. Your first withdrawal opens a short while after you join, once your first store has paid and cleared.

    Checked before they are paid

    NextaStore pays the mobile money fee, so you receive the full amount you withdraw. Withdrawals are checked before they are paid, which protects your earnings and the program.

    Fair play, in plain words

    • One store, one referrer. The first valid code or link a store used when signing up is the one credited.
    • Forgot to add your code? A store can add it within a few days of signing up, as long as nothing has been paid.
    • The store's first payment must be approved within a set window of signing up for the invite to count.
    • All rewards together on a first payment are capped at a fixed share of what the store paid. Bonuses are trimmed first if that ever applies.
    • Fake sign-ups, self-referrals and payments that are reversed are not rewarded, and may lead to the account being frozen.

    Circle or Crew?

    Both programs pay only when a store pays. Each account joins one of them.

    Questions people ask

    Do I need a store to join?

    No. Any verified NextaStore account can join Circle, including buyers. You only need a verified email and to accept the terms.

    Does it cost anything to join?

    No. Joining is free. You earn only when a store you invited pays for its Seller Pass.

    How do stores know I invited them?

    Through your invite link, your QR code or your personal code. The invite page tells the visitor who invited them (first name only), and the code is remembered on their device for a limited time.

    When can I withdraw?

    A reward is held for a few days after the payment is approved so it can clear. After that you can withdraw to your mobile money number once you reach the minimum of a small amount. Your very first withdrawal opens a short while after you join, once your first store has paid and cleared.

    What is the difference between Circle and Crew?

    Circle is open to anyone and works through your own network. Crew is a vetted field team that walks markets and meets shop owners in person, with higher rates that grow with rank.

    Can I be in both Circle and Crew?

    No. Each account is in one program at a time. Circle is open to anyone with a verified account; Crew is by application, so choose the one that fits how you want to work.
