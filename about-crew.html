    What is NextaStore Crew?

    Crew members are the face of NextaStore on the ground. You visit markets and shops in your areas, show owners how a NextaStore shop works on their own phone, and help them get started. In return you earn more than a Circle member, your rate rises as you bring in stores that stay, and you get a full set of field tools.

    Your own areas

    Choose the districts you work in and see which have the fewest live stores, so your time goes where it counts.

    A trail to climb

    A ladder of several ranks, from the first rank to the top rank. The more stores you bring in that stay, the higher your rate.

    Your leads are protected

    Log a prospect and it is yours for a set time, so a rival cannot take the credit for your visit.

    Works without signal

    No data in the market? Leads are saved on your phone and sent automatically when signal returns.

    How it works

    1. ApplyVerify your email, then give your phone number, choose up to a few areas and upload a clear photo of your ID and a plain photo of your face for your Crew ID card. Both are stored privately and are never shown publicly.
    2. Get approvedOur team reviews each application, within a few working days. You will be told when you are approved and your Crew tools unlock.
    3. Visit and log leadsMeet owners, run the short pitch and demo from your kit, and log each business so it is locked to you.
    4. Earn and rank upWhen a store you brought pays, you earn. Stores that stay and renew lift your rank and your rate.

    The ranks

    Everyone starts at the first rank. Bring in stores that pay and stay, and you move up the ladder.

      What you can earn

      Your first-month rate depends on your rank, from a growing percentage. Rewards are based on one month's Seller Pass price (a small monthly fee), even if the store prepays a year. Rank follows the stores that paid in the last few weeks, so you keep earning it.

      Long-commitment bonus

      + A bonus when a store's first payment covers several months or more.

      Stay bonus

      + A bonus when the store makes its first on-time renewal, within a set number of days after its paid period ends.

      Stocked-store bonus

      + A bonus with the stay bonus when the store still has at least a few products on its shelf.

      A promotion lands only while your quality score is healthy. Quality is behaviour, not volume: stores that pay, stay and renew, with no flags or reversals.

      Getting paid

      Rewards are paid to your mobile money number (MTN or Airtel). A reward is held for a few days after the store's payment is approved, so it can clear. Then it is yours to withdraw.

      Minimum withdrawal

      You can withdraw from a small minimum. Fewer, larger payouts keep things fast and fair for everyone.

      Paid within a few working days

      Once you request a withdrawal, it is paid to your number within a few working days. Your first withdrawal opens a short while after you join, once your first store has paid and cleared.

      Checked before they are paid

      NextaStore pays the mobile money fee, so you receive the full amount you withdraw. Withdrawals are checked before they are paid, which protects your earnings and the program.

      Fair play, in plain words

      • Crew places are approved by our team. We review each application and may ask for more detail.
      • One store, one referrer. Lead Lock protects honest visits; if the owner signs up with another member’s code during a live lock, an admin reviews it.
      • Everything is single level. You are paid only for stores you personally brought, and only for real payments.
      • All rewards on a first payment are capped at a fixed share of what the store paid, so the program stays sustainable for everyone.
      • Fake leads, backdated visits, duplicate sign-ups or reversed payments are not rewarded and can lead to removal from the Crew.

      Circle or Crew?

      Both programs pay only when a store pays. Each account joins one of them.

      Questions people ask

      Who can join the Crew?

      People who are comfortable meeting shop owners in person and know their local markets. You need a verified email, then you apply with your phone number, your areas and an ID photo, and our team approves each application.

      What is the Crew ID card?

      Once you are approved, you get a Crew ID card with your photo, name, rank and code, which you can save to your phone and show to shop owners. It unlocks after you have logged a few businesses, or sooner if our team releases it.

      Why do you need my ID?

      To keep the Crew trustworthy for shop owners and for you. The photo is stored privately, only the review team can see it, and it is never displayed publicly.

      Do I need data or a smartphone signal in the market?

      No. Leads you log offline are saved on your phone and sent automatically when you reconnect.

      How does ranking work?

      Your rank follows the number of stores that paid in the last few weeks, and a promotion needs a healthy quality score. Higher ranks earn a higher first-month rate.

      Can I also be in Circle?

      No, and you do not need to. Each account is in one program at a time. Crew includes the invite code, link and invite card that Circle offers, with higher rewards and extra field tools.

      Not ready to work the field? NextaStore Circle is open to anyone with a verified account and works through your own network.
